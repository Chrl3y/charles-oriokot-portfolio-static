About

Technology that holds up in real operations

My work sits where technology, finance and day-to-day operations meet. I support users and devices, but I also trace ledger faults, structure migrations, build management reporting, map payment flows and turn repeated operational work into governed automation.

I'm currently an IT Officer at Nova Microfinance. My portfolio spans core banking and loan-management support, data reconciliation, reporting platforms, payments and messaging integrations, access and device administration, vendor escalation, documentation, training and process improvement.

The thread across the work is simple: understand what the system is really doing, make the evidence visible, then leave behind a process that is safer and easier to run.

  • Based in: Kampala, Uganda
  • Current focus: Financial systems, data and automation
  • Working style: Evidence-led, practical and operations-aware
  • Languages: English, Ateso and basic Kumam

Smart Keys & Vehicle Immobilisation

Fleet Management Systems

GPS & Telematics Design

HR Systems

Dashboards & Reporting Systems

System & Data Migration

Payments & Mobile-Money Integration

Messaging & Notification Gateways

AI Agent & Knowledge Operations

IT & Systems Consultancy

Web Applications & Portals
