Technology, Community and Mobility

Home Blog September 8, 2026

Technology becomes meaningful when it improves the conditions around real work: the wellbeing of the people building it, the visibility a small business has over its stock, the confidence to protect an idea, or the ability of a rider to reach an opportunity.

Between April 2024 and December 2025, I used X to document events, conversations and working questions across Uganda's technology community. Read together, those posts tell a larger story about the kind of systems I want to help build: practical, responsible and close to everyday needs.

About the sources. The summaries below add professional context without changing the underlying record. Each card links to my original post on X, where the original wording, date, mentions and attached media remain available.
